# RAHP roadmap

## Current release boundary — v2.5.0 Psyche

v2.5.0 packages **Continuous Realization Assurance and Portable Adoption** as the stable post-v2.4 operating boundary.

The release does not change the stable engine/result/evidence compatibility families. It qualifies how RAHP operates continuously after an initial assessment: select the smallest complete materially affected proposition set, preserve valid evidence and explicit uncertainty, route executable/specialist work only where required, expose operational diagnostics without creating a second assurance truth, and reject performance optimizations that alter semantic reference inputs or normalized assurance behavior.

## Completed v2.5 tranche

1. **Realization-conformance discipline:** #799, #609, #691, #797 and #690 were reconciled against current source/runtime evidence without manufacturing closure where target or normative surfaces remain unavailable.
2. **Assurance-safe observability:** rahp-execution-telemetry/v1 records low-sensitivity operational timing, selection and lifecycle diagnostics while explicitly remaining non-authoritative for assurance outcomes.
3. **Performance regression control:** benchmark comparison is like-for-like, semantic-reference guarded, and tolerant of hosted-runner jitter; the canonical validator removes duplicate immutable YAML parsing.
4. **Non-DTG portability:** the maintained CAWG/C2PA family demonstrates first assessment, durable residuals, a real material source change, bounded reassessment and posture continuity using unchanged generic contracts.
5. **Documentation fidelity:** adoption, continuous assurance, telemetry, performance and current portfolio compatibility guidance are aligned to the qualified boundary.
6. **Research separation:** policy-as-subject work under #662/#668 remains experimental and outside the stable release.

## Current operating model

RAHP is **materiality-bounded by default**. Broader execution is not inherently stronger assurance.

A routine reassessment should:

- identify the materially affected proposition/evidence set;
- retain still-valid evidence with provenance;
- weaken, invalidate or leave unavailable evidence explicit;
- route specialists/evidence producers only where the proposition requires them;
- preserve source/runtime/normative distinctions;
- escalate to a full campaign/rebaseline when bounded scope cannot be defended.

Operational telemetry and performance data help explain how RAHP ran. They are not evidence that the assessed subject deserves PASS.

The repositories remain independently versioned and governed. Compatibility is contract- and evidence-based, not date-based.

## Capability continuity retained in v2.5

The v2.5 operating model is additive. It retains the earlier qualified capability families that remain part of the stable RAHP compatibility and documentation estate:

- **Durable assessment and finding lineage** preserves assessment identity, finding lineage and canonical identity across source changes and reassessment.
- **Governed remediation and retest** preserves remediation authority, acceptance criteria, closure evidence and retest lineage.
- **Assurance graph and impact analysis** keeps dependency-aware reachability and retest selection explicit.
- **Evidence provenance, freshness and delta** preserves source identity, freshness state and assurance delta rather than silently reusing stale evidence.
- **Executable authority and policy gates** keep authority scope, revocation and policy evaluation separately inspectable with INDETERMINATE preserved as a real outcome.
- **Portfolio and deployment presentation** projects bounded operational posture without becoming an alternative assurance source of truth.
- **Release qualification** continues to bind declared version, qualification contract, release decision, release-cut evidence and governed publication.

These capability names are retained deliberately because they are referenced by the stable capability-documentation registry and remain part of the toolkit's qualified historical continuity.

## Post-v2.5 priorities

1. **Realization evidence triggers:** reassess waiting-external/evidence-required owners only when their recorded upstream/runtime trigger fires.
2. **Longitudinal telemetry:** extend low-sensitivity phase/freshness/retained-vs-invalidated evidence diagnostics where they improve operations without creating assurance inference.
3. **Performance depth:** pursue additional measured hot-path reductions only with like-for-like semantic comparison and complete proposition coverage.
4. **Portable adoption:** add further non-DTG adopters without introducing consumer vocabulary into the generic core.
5. **Resilience depth:** exercise higher DRARM assurance levels with attributable runtime and operational evidence producers.
6. **Composition/redress:** deepen contestability, correction, continuation and closure evidence across composed trust decisions.
7. **Research graduation:** continue #662/#668 independently; stable integration requires its own evidence-backed graduation decision.

## Non-regression rules

- No human-only controller transition.
- Missing evidence never becomes PASS.
- Workflow success never substitutes for assurance success.
- Model and evidence gaps remain explicit residuals.
- Replay remains idempotent; new pins create lineage.
- Static/source, synthetic, runtime and governance evidence remain distinct.
- Normative convergence never silently becomes implementation conformance.
- Component PASS never implies composition PASS.
- Source findings are not silently reinterpreted to fit a local catalogue.
- Generic RAHP core remains independent of DTG, OpenVTC, CAWG, A2A or any other consumer vocabulary.
- Broader execution is not treated as stronger assurance by definition; reassessment scope follows material proposition coverage.
- Non-selected evidence is never silently refreshed or promoted to PASS.

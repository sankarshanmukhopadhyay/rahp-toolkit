# Bounded completeness and semantic coverage

RAHP does not claim universal completeness. Review readiness means that the propositions material to the advertised stable boundary are identifiable, have an evidence route, and preserve unsupported or unresolved states without converting them into positive assurance.

| Proposition family | Current coverage | Evidence route | Boundary / unsupported surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| Required evidence | covered | engine contract, negative fixtures, review claim ledger | completeness of external evidence is not inferred |
| Workflow vs assurance | covered | engine contract and controller validation | successful execution is never sufficient for GREEN |
| Component vs composition | covered | composition negative fixtures and cross-spec examples | novel compositions require fresh composition evidence |
| Freshness / invalidation | covered | freshness-delta validation and resolution fixtures | external change detection is bounded by configured sources |
| Authority / recognition | covered | authority-policy gates and resolution fixtures | cryptographic validity does not establish current authority |
| Independence / quorum | covered | false-independence and quorum-capture examples | organizational independence may require external evidence |
| Specialist handoff | covered at contract boundary | DPIP handoff tests and specialist fixtures | specialist domain conclusions remain specialist-owned |
| Provenance / source pin | covered at contract boundary | source-pinned review records and engine invariants | authenticity of external facts is not manufactured by RAHP |
| Human-power / redress | represented | bounded reconciliation evidence | deployment effectiveness requires realization evidence |
| Resource exhaustion | partially covered | DRARM recursion fixture | comprehensive adversarial size/time/memory qualification is not established |
| Dependency / supply chain | partially covered | CI install and workflow governance | dependencies are not bit-reproducibly locked |
| Real-world deployment effectiveness | evidence-required | deployment evidence manifests where available | synthetic fixtures are not realization evidence |

## Completeness criteria

A proposition family is **covered** only when its rule is explicit and at least one executable or source-pinned evidence route exercises it. **Partially covered** means a material boundary is represented but meaningful classes remain unqualified. **Evidence-required** means RAHP can represent the question but cannot answer it without deployment or external evidence.

Portfolio completeness is therefore a statement about traceable proposition coverage, not about the universe of possible harms, attacks, deployments or governance failures. New material propositions, source changes, compositions or specialist obligations reopen assessment rather than inheriting completeness from this table.

## Release scope

v2.8.0 packages the post-v2.7 work into a new additive assurance baseline. It does not change the stable engine contract family, normalized result schema or evidence-retention contract.

### Comparative assurance

RAHP can compare arbitrary source-pinned assessment/release sets through a generic comparative-assurance contract rather than a Dogwood/Eucalyptus-specific path. The implementation preserves comparability boundaries, finding-level differences, supported judgments and explicit non-comparable states.

### Adversarial assurance and reviewability

The release adds bounded adversarial assurance qualification, cross-boundary challenge cases, an execution ledger and reviewer protocol. Material assurance changes now have stronger contributor/reviewer guidance for claim, implementation, evidence, reproduction, limitation and challenge.

### Agent authority and continuity

New agent-authority and continuity checks preserve the distinction between identity, lineage and actionable authority. Agent or delegation evidence cannot silently become authorization, and replacement/retry continuity remains independently inspectable.

### Lifecycle-continuation authority

A new portable lifecycle-continuation assurance pattern covers renewal, reissuance and successor creation without introducing a duplicate renewal-specific risk family. Historical validity does not imply current continuation authority.

### Consumer-interaction evidence

Wallets, VTAs, browsers, relying-party applications and agent interfaces can produce framework-neutral interaction evidence through the existing `rahp-evidence-producer-result/v1` envelope. RAHP remains independent of Playwright, Selenium, Appium and other execution frameworks. UI success does not establish protocol correctness, authorization or terminal assurance.

### Observability-induced harm

Synthetic DTFC observability vectors and a bounded evaluator make cross-context trace correlation and telemetry-to-authority promotion testable without claiming a real-world incident or deployment-wide privacy result.

### Clean-room and deployment evidence

The Eucalyptus clean-room campaign and native-evidence package provide source-pinned component/composition evidence and preserve missing evidence as explicit residual state. These artifacts are bounded to their captured revisions and do not certify a deployment.

### Release engineering

Release publication now has stronger declaration-driven preflight/postflight verification, idempotent tag/release reconciliation and explicit evidence that a published release matches its declaration and remains the latest release pointer.

## Compatibility

No breaking change is declared.

The stable compatibility authority remains:

```text
rahp-engine-contract-v1 revision 1.3
normalized result schema version 1
rahp-evidence-retention-v1
```

Existing conforming v2.7 consumers do not require schema migration solely to consume v2.8.

## Known limitations

- Independent external adoption remains **NOT_YET_TESTED**.
- Consumer-interaction tests establish portable contract behavior, not live wallet/VTA/OpenVTC deployment assurance.
- OpenVTC runtime evidence tracked by #994 remains external and may remain INDETERMINATE.
- Eucalyptus evidence is source-pinned and bounded; it is not certification or a deployment-wide conformance claim.
- Fiduciary assurance material remains research-oriented and is not promoted to the stable capability boundary by this release.
- Synthetic observability vectors do not establish a verified privacy incident.

## Migration

No migration is required for the stable engine/result/evidence contracts. Consumers that choose to emit consumer-interaction evidence should use the portable producer-result contract and keep tool-specific observations inside `evidence.observations[]`.

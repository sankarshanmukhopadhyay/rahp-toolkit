# RAHP Toolkit v2.6.0 — Commander

**Release date:** 2026-10-01  
**Theme:** Full-Stack Assurance Orchestration and Explicit Evidence State  
**Codename:** Commander (*Moduza procris*)

## Release summary

v2.6.0 packages the post-v2.5 stable work into a coherent additive RAHP capability boundary. The central change is that orchestration completion, assurance outcome, and evidence maturity are now independently observable. A campaign may complete while assurance remains INDETERMINATE, but a material assurance lens or required evidence obligation may not silently disappear.

The stable engine/result/evidence compatibility families remain unchanged.

## Full-stack assurance orchestration

RAHP now represents full-stack campaigns using independent `process_state`, `assurance_state`, and `evidence_maturity` dimensions. Explicit lens dispositions cover RAHP, security, composition, DRARM, and specialist assessment.

A material lens may terminate as required-but-not-executed / INDETERMINATE when evidence or a legitimate producer is unavailable. Workflow completion is never promoted into assurance success.

## Attributable evidence attempts

Required evidence reuses the `rahp-evidence-probe-ledger/v1` discipline. Each required evidence item must be attributable as EXECUTED, ATTEMPTED_UNAVAILABLE, or NO_APPLICABLE_PRODUCER. An omitted probe is an orchestration defect.

This makes evidence absence inspectable without manufacturing evidence or weakening the terminal state.

## Composition, resilience, and migration

Portable version-skew reasoning derives old/new producer-consumer combinations and requires mixed-version behavior to be compatible, explicitly refused, or unspecified. Unspecified behavior remains an indeterminate/model-gap proposition rather than an inferred defect.

DRARM applicability is synchronized into the lens ledger. Resilience silence is invalid; material resilience work must be executed, explicitly not applicable with provenance, or retained as required but not executed.

## Privacy catalogue advances

The portable catalogue adds reusable mechanisms for proof-level correlation, status-query observability, post-disclosure purpose violation, authoritative privacy-control subversion, and verification-artefact retention concentration, with corresponding controls, guardrails, regression evidence, and source/judgment separation.

These mechanisms do not replace specialist privacy assessment where specialist evidence is required.

## Portfolio and realization hardening

The post-v2.5 tranche also binds portfolio assurance routing to exact snapshot content, tightens DTG/VSC routing and residual normalization, and reconciles task-citation realization boundaries without treating normative convergence as implementation conformance.

## Execution engineering

The release incorporates measured profiling, scale characterization, pressure-test batching, and the documented decision not to introduce a native execution kernel at the current evidence boundary. Performance evidence remains engineering evidence and cannot set an assurance outcome.

## LPC assurance artifacts

The LPC pre-demo work now has an explicit scope manifest, evidence templates, and assurance record. Those artifacts helped harden the portable full-stack orchestration contract; the LPC-specific subject does not become a dependency of the generic core.

## Experimental research boundary

Policy-as-assurance-subject work under #662/#667 remains experimental. Decision-resolution research under PR #817 also remains experimental with provisional identifiers outside the stable catalogue. Neither is promoted by v2.6.0.

## Assurance and compatibility invariants

v2.6.0 preserves and strengthens these rules:

- missing evidence never becomes PASS;
- workflow success never becomes assurance success;
- component PASS never implies composition PASS;
- normative convergence never silently becomes implementation conformance;
- non-selected evidence is never silently refreshed;
- every material configured lens receives an explicit disposition;
- every required evidence obligation receives an attributable attempt state;
- applicable unexecuted lenses remain INDETERMINATE rather than PASS;
- DRARM silence is invalid where resilience is material;
- experimental research is not silently graduated.

## Compatibility

No compatibility-family break is introduced:

- `rahp-engine-contract-v1`, revision `1.3`;
- normalized result schema `1`;
- `rahp-evidence-retention-v1`.

## Upgrade guidance

Existing v2.5 adopters can upgrade without changing the stable engine/result/evidence contract families. Consumers using full-stack orchestration should treat process completion, assurance outcome, and evidence maturity as separate claims; explicitly disposition every configured material lens; and ensure required evidence obligations have attributable probe-attempt states.

## Threat-model interpretation and proportionate findings

- **Configuration carrying authorization vs creating authority:** a DID delivered in the provisioning overlay can carry the operator's prior decision if that decision has an independently accountable origin and is bound to the right attested node. A configured DID alone proves neither governing authorization nor that host-selected deployment was approved. RAHP cannot declare an offline-writer exception without VTI #64.
- **No new registrar required by default:** an offline operator-authorized DID plus attested configuration, target-specific signature, one-use claim and traceable hand-off *could* be sufficient under an explicit threat model. A mandatory online registrar, additional consensus service or new RAHP protocol is not justified by reviewed evidence.
- **Rollback and image-substitution:** source-present locking/sentinel is not evidence that hostile-host storage rollback cannot recreate state. Pinning PCR0 and comparing VTA DID/quote matters; `--vta-url` intentionally sacrifices independent node-identity binding and must be explicitly disclosed.
- **Hand-off wire carriage:** `handoffRollover` is ecosystem-specific and requires upstream spec/Task judgment. Technically successful rollover is not proof of canonical protocol conformity.

## A. Definitions

An "Account" represents your legal relationship with GitHub.

The “Agreement” refers, collectively, to all the terms, conditions, notices contained or referenced in this document and all other operating rules, policies and procedures that GitHub may publish from time to time on the Website.

The “Service” refers to the applications, software, products, and services provided by GitHub, including through the Website and any Beta Previews.

The “Website” refers to GitHub’s website located at github.com and GitHub-owned subdomains and product websites. Occasionally, websites owned by GitHub may provide different or additional terms of service. If those additional terms conflict with this Agreement, the more specific terms apply to the relevant page or service.

## B. Account Terms

### 5. Additional Terms

In some situations, third parties' terms may apply to your use of GitHub. These Terms remain GitHub's agreement with you, while other parties' terms govern their relationships with you.

If you are a government User or otherwise accessing or using a GitHub Service in a government capacity, the Government Amendment to GitHub Terms of Service applies to you.

If you have signed up for GitHub Enterprise Cloud, the Enterprise Cloud Addendum applies to you.

## C. Acceptable Use

You agree that you will not under any circumstances violate our [Acceptable Use Policies](/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies) or [Community Guidelines](/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
